# Write a product requirements document

Use only decisions and facts established in the conversation or verified from the project. Do not reopen settled decisions or invent product requirements.

Include:

- Goal
- Non-goals
- Target users and the problem being solved
- User-visible behavior and workflows
- Requirements and constraints
- Acceptance criteria
- Resolved decisions and their rationale
- Open questions only when the user explicitly left them open

Keep the document product-focused. Do not include implementation steps, file names, or engineering design unless they are themselves product constraints.
